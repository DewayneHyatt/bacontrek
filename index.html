The situation

Somewhere in the Gamma Quadrant, a routine supply run just became a full-blown crisis: the fleet's sole certified bacon supplier has been assimilated by the Borg, with a major diplomatic gala only 26 days out. What follows is the real (if slightly ridiculous) paper trail of how the crisis got solved — a meeting transcript, a supply chain spreadsheet, and a report template. You'll create the slide deck yourself.

Your job: work through it the way you would a real business crisis, using Copilot to do the heavy lifting at every step. You'll turn a live meeting into notes, a messy spreadsheet into insight, a blank template into a finished report, and that report into an executive-ready deck — in about 20 minutes, start to finish.

This isn't a scripted demo

This isn't a scripted demo. Every prompt here is one you can actually run, on the actual files, and get a real result. Copy it, paste it, watch what happens. If you want to go off-script and try your own prompt instead, do it — that's the point.

What you'll need: just this page and the three source files in the starting kit below. No login beyond your own Copilot license. Work through all four stations in one sitting, or bookmark this page and come back whenever — nothing here expires.
